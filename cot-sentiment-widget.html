    How the score works

    Each market gets a score from −100 (bearish) to +100 (bullish), built from the latest weekly CFTC Commitments of Traders report and the 20-week price range.

    Price position: discount (lower half of the 20-week range) or premium (upper half)35 pts
    Institutions over 4 weeks: Managed Money for commodities, Asset Managers for financials30 pts
    Fast money this week: Swap Dealers for commodities, Leveraged Funds for financials; positions at an extreme count against the trend20 pts
    Open interest change, read together with institutional flow15 pts

    Verdict: +40 and above Bullish, +15 Lean Bullish, −15 to +15 Neutral, −40 Lean Bearish, below that Bearish.

    Data: CFTC Disaggregated and Traders in Financial Futures reports, Yahoo Finance and Coinbase prices. COT data is released every Friday for the prior Tuesday.
