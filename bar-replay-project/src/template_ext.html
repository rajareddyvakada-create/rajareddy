UT Bot signals

Signals are calculated on the timeframe you're viewing and appear only after the candle closes.

CBDR ranges (New York time)

Binance and Twelve Data times are converted exactly. For a CSV, enter the UTC offset its times were exported in (many MT5 brokers use UTC+2 or UTC+3; check yours). Ranges appear once their window has closed.

CBDR entry model

Long: price takes out the CBDR low, then a candle closes back above it. Entry at that close, stop below the sweep low, target at the CBDR high plus the SD. Short is the mirror image. One setup per range, looked for until 12:00 New York time the next day. Setups are graphics only; they don't open practice trades.

Practice trades

Orders fill at the close of the last candle shown. If one candle touches both the stop-loss and the target, it counts as a loss.
